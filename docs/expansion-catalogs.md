# Additional core compendiums

Rules authority: the user's 10,000 Swords Against Heaven 2.0.3 PDF. Rebuild these source catalogs with `tools/extract-expansion.py`, then compile the packs with `node tools/build-packs.mjs`.

## World Being Traits

All 16 entries in the 3d6 table on p.213 are in **World Being Traits**. They use the Trait item type and carry searchable World Being tags. The book allows exceptions to its usual restriction on Wanderers, so this is not enforced as a hard sheet restriction.

Deity and Chthonian grant their extra Health/Hale levels and Cultivations. Mystical increases maximum Ardor; Exdead increases maximum Health; Demon adds its unconditional attack bonus. Conditional numeric bonuses have separate, initially disabled Active Effects; enable them only for the qualifying check or attack. Narrative abilities, Holy vulnerability, immunities, triggered healing, Dragon Eye's fourth Action, and Rage's additional Action are preserved as manual rules. The printed Rage wording is ambiguous ("+1 Action per Round instead of 3"); no replacement Action total is invented.

## Sect Fashion & Gear

All 34 equipment cards on pp.127–185 are represented, organized by **Sect → Fashion / Weapons / Gear / Accessories**. There are 37 entries because Houyi Tonggong has four selectable forms: Bow, Crossbow, Launcher, and Gun. Claim one form, not all four.

Fashion carries its printed Armor and Ardor. Weapons use the base weapon statistics from pp.100–109 plus their printed inherent modifiers and affinities. Houyi Tonggong has a total +4 attack bonus including Artifact, twice its chosen base range, Burn damage, Unblockable, and no ammunition requirement. Nezha Huojianqiang has Ruination damage and no ammunition requirement. Carry costs follow the printed square symbols, including three slots for the Five Thunder Japamala. N/A prices are preserved in descriptions; numeric Mag cost 0 is a placeholder, not a free purchase.

Hermetic Robes have their Magick-area effect, Liver-Eater's Raiment its movement increase, Mendicant Begging Garb its Health increase, and Phoenix Feather Cap its Ardor increase. Other conditional bonuses, auras, special actions, alternate weapon attacks, charges, summons, and narrative effects retain the full card text for manual resolution. These imports do not claim to automate every equipment ability.

## Jobs

All 12 Jobs on pp.42–43 are organized into **Body** and **Mind**. Eleven fixed Jobs have their printed Cultivations and 35 independent starting-item snapshots, including Traits and paired weapons. Dropping one on a sheet uses the existing Job replacement behavior: previous Job-granted items are replaced, manually added items and Cultivation adjustments remain.

New grants start unequipped. Equip the desired weapons and Fashion after applying the Job. Conditional Traits retain the Grimoire's inactive defaults. Hunter's Longbow tracks its 12 arrows and Cryptomancer's Pistol tracks its 10 bullets directly on the weapon. Named starter weapons inherit their printed base weapon type and explicit modifiers, rather than borrowing a different commercial weapon's statistics. The Occult Keris' return-on-throw rule remains manual.

Wretched is a customizable template. Before dropping it, assign the printed 4/3/2/2/1/1 spread and add the chosen weapon, Common/Uncommon Fashion, and Trait to its loadout. The template blocks application until these choices exist; its initial six 1s are placeholders. Roll and enter 6+d6 ammunition for a chosen ranged weapon. Pugilist's printed spread totals 14 and is retained without silently normalizing it. Hunter's Garb uses its full Fashion entry's 1 Armor (p.110), which the abbreviated Hunter Job listing omits.

All eight system compendiums are included in every complete release ZIP. No separate download is needed.
