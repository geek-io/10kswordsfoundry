Core symbols for Rules 2.1 (Physique, Motorics, Mind, Wits, Face, Heart), extracted from the user-supplied 10,000 Swords Against Heaven 2.1 rulebook (final 423-page edition), PDF pages 28–29. File names are the system's Core keys (`voc` = Face, `spi` = Heart). Artwork remains the property of the rulebook creators.

The PDF stores the three pictures of p.29 in the order Heart, Wits, Face, not the printed order; they were matched to the Cores by the printed colour (Wits orange, Face green, Heart pink).

The pictures keep their printed size (about 162 × 242 px) and are saved as 32-colour PNGs with transparency; only anti-aliased edge pixels differ slightly from the PDF's images.
